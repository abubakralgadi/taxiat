# TAXIAT — art direction and interaction system

## Position
An Arabic-first architectural editorial experience. Architecture and material surfaces carry the page; the AI studio helps turn interest into a decision. The identity uses black, white, and a restrained deep red accent sampled from TAXIAT's Instagram profile and story highlights.

## Brand source
- The displayed TAXIAT wordmark is the profile image from [@taxiat_co](https://www.instagram.com/taxiat_co/), saved locally at `client/public/images/taxiat-logo-instagram.jpg`. It is a 150 px raster reference; replace it with the original vector artwork when available.
- Red `#9e1d21` is sampled from a repeated solid story-highlight background. It is an observed screen color, not a published brand specification.

## Composition
- A full-bleed architectural opening with one short proposition and two clear paths.
- A generous material atlas with one large visual surface and an adjacent index. Selection changes the feature area and connects to the studio.
- The current page moves from the architectural introduction to the studio, material explorer, work steps, and contact. The project stories section was removed at the user's request.
- The studio presents the uploaded building and final comparison at architectural scale. Its progress reflects actual request stages, not a simulated countdown.

## Type, spacing, motion
- Alexandria, with IBM Plex Sans Arabic as fallback, for Arabic display and reading; Space Grotesk for Latin codes and numbers.
- Fluid display sizes, readable Arabic line heights, a consistent 8px spacing base, and strong right alignment.
- Motion tokens: quick 180ms, standard 320ms, reveal 680ms; one restrained ease-out curve. Reveal and scanning effects stop under reduced-motion preference.
- Focus states, touch targets, clear errors, and accurate result labels take priority over visual effects.

## Content integrity
The local facade images are concept imagery. They must not be presented as completed TAXIAT projects or actual AI outputs. TAXIAT's public catalog confirms WPC codes US09, US30, US31, UH46 and UH61, the HPL code 10002, and the two named Travertine colors used here. Stone veneer and fiber cement are shown as families without a claimed SKU; their exact finishes need catalog photography and verification before publication.
